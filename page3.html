About Us

Since our establishment, Africa Security Agency has focused on providing dependable security services that build long-term partnerships with clients across multiple industries.

Who We Are

Founded in 2017 as a response company serving the residential market in Cape Town, Africa Security Agency has grown into a complete security solutions provider serving clients throughout the Western Cape and Gauteng.


Our customised services are designed to meet each client's unique operational requirements while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism.

Why Clients Choose ASA

Our directors remain actively involved in every client relationship, providing direct oversight and ensuring exceptional service delivery.


Managers and supervisors work closely with our guards, supporting their development while ensuring clients receive dependable, proactive security services every day.

Our Mission

To provide holistic, tailor-made security solutions based on each client's unique business profile while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, integrity and service excellence.
